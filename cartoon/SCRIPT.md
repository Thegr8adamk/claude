# Walt's Way Home

A 5-minute Pixar-style animated short.

## Characters

| Name | What | Look | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Walt** | Lost hiker, 50s | Chubby and round-faced. Bushy gray mustache, round glasses, khaki bucket hat, red plaid flannel shirt, tan cargo shorts, big brown boots, oversized orange backpack | Warm, a bit clumsy, talks to himself |
| **Pip** | Red squirrel | Tiny, huge fluffy tail, big shiny eyes, one chipped front tooth | Fast, excited, squeaky |
| **Hazel** | Barn owl | Heart-shaped white face, half-closed wise eyes, ruffled "eyebrow" feathers | Slow, calm, old |
| **Bramble** | Black bear | Very big, round belly, soft brown muzzle, kind eyes. Shy | Deep, soft, gentle |
| **Fireflies** | A swarm | Small warm lights | No voice, only chimes |
